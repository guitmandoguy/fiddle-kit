      New song

      1 · Song details

      Screenshot your Strum Machine chart and choose it here; set beats per bar first. Or type it: bars split with |, one chord per bar or one per beat, / repeats the last chord. B, E7, F#m, Bb all work.

      While you sing and tap, the next word shows on the tap pad. Words are matched to notes in order.

      2 · Record the melody

      Guitar plays your chart at the tempo above. Sing the melody and hold the pad down for as long as each note lasts: press as the word starts, let go when it ends. During the first bar of clicks, tap along with the clicks (that measures your headphone delay). If the song has pickup notes, sing them in the second bar of clicks. Wear headphones so the microphone only hears you.

      Sing the melody however feels natural: any key, any speed, slow down wherever you like. Tap the pad once as each word starts. A word you hold across two notes gets two taps. No backing plays, so any headphones (or none) work. The app fits what you sang to your chart's bars and key.

      The app plays 4 count-in clicks with the key note under them, then boom-chuck guitar from your chart. Sing the melody over it with headphones on, so the microphone only hears you. If the song starts with pickup notes, sing them during the end of the count-in.

      Nothing plays while you record, so this works with any headphones or none. Tap Hear the key to get your starting pitch, then Record. You get 4 clicks with the key note under them (if your headphones keep playing), plus big numbers to count along with. Start singing on bar 1; pickup notes go at the end of the count-in.

      Tuning

      Singer's tuning is what experienced groups do: pure fifths and octaves, major thirds pulled low, everything else equal. Full just rings hardest. Equal matches the guitar exactly.

      Passing notes

      Pick a song and your part, then work up the steps. Each step gets harder; move on when you're matching most notes.
